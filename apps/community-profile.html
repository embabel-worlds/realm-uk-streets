How it works

1. The data

Everything on this page is fetched from the publisher when the view runs, cached at the publisher's own cadence, and never copied into the world. The place is the only thing stored: a UkPlace with its postcode resolved once into the codes each register keys on.

  • OHID Fingertips — fourteen indicators (school readiness, youth-justice first-time entrants, NEET, children in low income, under-18 conceptions, Year 6 overweight, smoking at delivery, low birth weight, MMR, suicide, life expectancy, employment, disability-free life expectancy) as AreaIndicator, keyed on the upper-tier authority code; cached a day. The "compared to England" column is the publisher's significance test.
  • NOMIS — claimant count (monthly) and mid-year population by age band; cached a day and a week.
  • MHCLG Indices of Deprivation 2025 — lower-tier district summaries; cached a week.
  • DfE Explore Education Statistics — the early years foundation stage profile as SchoolReadiness; cached a day.
  • Charity Commission — the daily bulk register: area of operation (by upper-tier authority), the charity row, the event history; each row carries the Commission's own extract date.
  • 360Giving GrantNav — every published grant into the district as GrantIntoPlace; one file per district per day.

2. The questions

Each panel is one saved view, invoked by name with the place as its parameter. The Cypher shown below is read back from the server when this section opens — it is the query that ran, not a copy.

3. When it looks wrong

  • A blank tile or an empty panel means the publisher holds no figure for this authority — a place in a two-tier district reads its county's children's figures and the profile names that county; it is not a zero.
  • No charities almost always means the Commission spells the authority differently from the place's stored name (it records operation by county or unitary, under its own spelling).
  • Figures that differ from the publisher's site inside a day are the cache, not a bug; the footer states when each source was read.
  • The "stands in England" list shrinks as the dial rises by design: it is a minimum extremity, and an authority near the national middle on everything shows nothing at 80.

4. What made this possible

  • The "smart insight" is a rank, a share and an arithmetic distance computed inside the query over every authority's rows — the polarity is declared per indicator in the view, and the standard-error distance uses the publisher's own confidence interval. Nothing is scored by a model and nothing is opaque: the Cypher in section 2 is the whole method.
  • The grants, charity and statistics panels are joins across four publishers on a code the place resolved once; on a conventional stack each is a nightly copy with its own staleness.
  • The Charity Commission's zipped tab-delimited register and the DfE's gzip-encoded CSV are read by the engine's tabular producer as they are published; no ETL was written for this page.
  • This page asks the world by view name at load; there is no backend of its own to deploy or patch.
